The [Griddles](/griddles/) hub is a good place to start if you're still sorting out the broader setup, but if you've already got the griddle and want to cook better food with less hassle, this list is where to focus.

The five accessories here cover the full range: toolkits, seasoning, cleaning, and surface protection. Some serve every griddle cook regardless of experience level. Others solve a specific problem, and if that's not your problem, they're not your product. I'll tell you which is which.

## What to Look For in Blackstone Griddle Accessories

### Material and Surface Compatibility

The material an accessory is made from matters more on a flat-top griddle than it does on a grate-style grill. Griddle surfaces are seasoned steel , scratch it with the wrong tool and you're undoing months of polymerized oil buildup. Metal spatulas are fine on raw steel; on a seasoned surface that's developing a good nonstick layer, silicone or well-finished stainless edges are worth the slight tradeoff in rigidity.

Cast iron accessories , like a separate cast iron griddle plate , bring their own seasoning considerations. They need the same care as the griddle itself: dry them fast, oil them thin, store them somewhere they won't rust. The payoff is a cooking surface that holds heat exceptionally well and only gets better with use.

### Kit Completeness vs. Kit Bloat

A 35-piece kit sounds like a lot. Some of those pieces earn their space; others are there to make the number look impressive on the box. Before buying a kit, think through your actual cooking , breakfast spreads, smash burgers, stir-fry, hibachi at home , and check whether the kit addresses those use cases specifically.

The tools that do real work on a flat top: wide spatulas (for flipping, moving food, scraping), a basting cover (for trapping steam and melting cheese), and a scraper (for post-cook cleanup). Everything else is situational. A kit that includes all three of those in solid form is more valuable than one that includes twelve specialty tools you'll use twice.

### Cleaning and Maintenance Scope

Flat-top cooking produces great food and a serious mess. Grease, food bits, and carbon buildup accumulate every session. The right cleaning kit isn't optional , it's the difference between a seasoning that builds over years and a surface that corrodes and sticks. Griddle stones, scrapers, and scouring pads each handle a different layer of that cleanup job, and having the right tool for each keeps the surface in shape without stripping the seasoning.

Seasoning conditioner is a separate category from cleaning tools, but it belongs in the same conversation. After you clean, you re-oil. Using a purpose-formulated conditioner rather than a random cooking oil gives you a more consistent result and extends the life of the seasoning layer considerably. The full range of [griddle](/griddles/) maintenance products is worth understanding before you settle into a routine.

### Heat and Safety Considerations

A griddle runs hot , typically 400°F to 550°F across the surface during heavy cooking , and anything that comes near that surface needs to handle the heat. Silicone tools rated for high temperatures are safer than painted or plastic-handled options with ambiguous temperature ratings. Check the rated temperature before you assume a silicone product is adequate for flat-top work.

Counter mats and surface protection accessories have a different heat requirement: they sit away from the direct cooking surface and manage splatter, drip, and condensation rather than direct flame contact. Those products typically don't need to be rated as high, but the materials still matter. Cheap silicone degrades faster and can leave residue on the surfaces you're trying to protect.

## Buying Guide

### Matching Accessories to Your Griddle Setup

Not every accessory on this list is relevant to every griddle cook. The first filter is what you're actually cooking on. A Blackstone 36" four-burner setup has different needs than a Camp Chef two-burner, and both are different from someone using a cast iron plate on top of a gas grill. The 35-piece kit and the Blackstone seasoning conditioner target dedicated flat-top griddle owners. The Utheer cast iron plate targets grill owners who want griddle capability without a second dedicated unit.

Figure out your actual setup before you start accumulating accessories. Buying a cleaning kit designed for Blackstone geometry when you're cooking on a different brand's surface isn't a mistake exactly, but it's imprecise. Browse the full [griddle accessories](/griddles/) range with your specific surface in mind.

### Prioritizing the First Three Accessories

New griddle cooks tend to buy the cooking tools first and figure out cleaning and seasoning later. That order makes sense for the first few sessions, but it creates problems quickly. Cleaning and seasoning should be in place before your second cook. The seasoning conditioner ensures you're maintaining the surface correctly from the start. The cleaning kit ensures you're not improvising a cleanup method that damages what you're building.

The cooking tools , spatulas, basting cover, scraper , are necessary from session one. If you're buying a kit, confirm it includes those three in full-size versions. Undersized spatulas on a large griddle surface make maneuvering food genuinely harder, not just slightly inconvenient.

### Silicone vs. Metal for Flat-Top Tools

Metal spatulas on seasoned flat-top surfaces are a point of ongoing debate among griddle cooks. The honest answer is that properly polymerized seasoning on carbon steel is durable enough to handle metal tools without damage, and many experienced griddle cooks use metal exclusively. Where silicone earns its place is in the early sessions before the seasoning has fully hardened, and on cast iron surfaces where the seasoning layer tends to be slightly thinner.

The scratch-free silicone tools included with the Utheer cast iron kit are the right match for that specific surface. On a well-seasoned Blackstone, metal spatulas offer better feedback and edge performance. Neither is universally correct , match the tool material to the surface condition.

### Cleaning Cadence and Surface Longevity

A griddle surface is only as good as the maintenance routine behind it. The cleaning kit handles the session-by-session cleanup. The seasoning conditioner handles the rebuild after cleaning. Done consistently, this cycle builds a surface that gets more non-stick with age rather than less. Skipped or improvised, it leads to sticky spots, rust patches, and uneven heat distribution.

The GRILLART cleaning kit handles the mechanical side of this well. Pair it with the Blackstone conditioner and you have the full maintenance loop covered. That combination , systematic cleaning followed by thin re-oiling , is what separates a griddle that cooks well for years from one that needs resurfacing by the second season.

### When to Buy a Bundle vs. Individual Tools

Bundles make financial sense when you're starting from zero and lack the context to know which individual tools you'd choose. The 35-piece kit is that purchase , it covers the bases without requiring prior knowledge of what the bases are. As your cooking develops and you identify specific gaps (a longer-handled spatula, a second basting cover, a different scraper geometry), buying individual replacements becomes more precise.

Single-purpose products like the seasoning conditioner and the silicone mat solve specific problems. They belong in the cart when you've identified that specific problem, not speculatively. Buy the kit first, identify the gaps, then fill them deliberately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need special accessories for a Blackstone griddle, or will standard grill tools work?

Standard grill tools designed for grate cooking , tong-style tools, thin grill forks, wire brushes , aren't well-suited for flat-top work. You need wide spatulas to move food across a solid surface, a basting cover to trap steam, and a flat scraper for cleanup. The [35PCS Griddle Accessories Kit](product:35pcs-griddle-accessories-kit-flat) covers those specific needs in one purchase and is compatible with Blackstone surfaces. The geometry of flat-top cooking is different enough from grate grilling that the right tools genuinely matter.
### How often do I need to season my Blackstone griddle?

Every cook starts with a light oil application and ends with a thin re-coat after cleaning. Beyond that session-by-session maintenance, a full re-seasoning , multiple thin coats built up over heat , is needed whenever the surface shows dry spots, rust, or sticky patches. The [BLACKSTONE 4146 Griddle Seasoning and Cast Iron Conditioner](product:blackstone-4146-griddle-seasoning-and) is formulated for both the initial seasoning build and the ongoing maintenance coat. Consistency matters more than frequency , a thin coat after every cook compounds into excellent seasoning over time.
### Is the Utheer cast iron griddle plate a replacement for a dedicated Blackstone, or a supplement?

It's a supplement, not a replacement. The Utheer plate converts a gas grill into a functional flat top and works well for that purpose, but it doesn't replicate the dedicated burner control, grease management system, or cooking station design of a standalone Blackstone. If you own a gas grill and want occasional griddle cooking without the footprint or cost of a second unit, the [Utheer Griddle Kit](product:utheer-griddle-kit-25-x) makes sense. If flat-top cooking is going to be a regular part of your routine, a dedicated griddle is the better long-term investment.
### What's the right way to clean a Blackstone without damaging the seasoning?

Clean the surface while it's still warm , not screaming hot, but warm enough that food debris lifts easily. Use a scraper to push debris toward the grease trap, then work the surface with a griddle stone or scouring pad for stubborn buildup. Avoid soap and chemical cleaners, which strip the seasoning. The [GRILLART Griddle Cleaning Kit](product:grillart-griddle-cleaning-kit-for) includes the scraper, grill stone, and scouring pad needed to handle this sequence correctly without reaching for anything that damages the surface.
### What is the silicone grill mat actually used for, and do I need one?
The mat sits on your prep counter or side table adjacent to the griddle , it's not a cooking surface. It protects the counter from heat, grease, and splatter, and gives you a designated resting spot for spatulas and tools between uses. The [Silicone Grill Mat](product:silicone-grill-mat-compatible-with) is a quality-of-life purchase rather than a functional necessity.
