Getting pizza off a gas or charcoal grill is one of those backyard goals that looks simple until you're staring at a burnt bottom and raw cheese. The right stone changes the equation entirely , and if you're running a Weber, there are purpose-built options that integrate directly with your existing setup rather than requiring workarounds. These are the [pizza ovens](/pizza-ovens/) accessories worth knowing about before you buy.

Not all stones are equal, and the gap between a good choice and a frustrating one usually comes down to material, fit, and heat management. Understanding those three factors before you commit will save you a ruined crust and a trip back to the return desk.

## What to Look For in a Weber Grill Pizza Stone

### Material and Heat Retention

The stone's material is the single biggest determinant of crust quality. Cordierite is the benchmark , it handles thermal shock well, meaning you can move it from a cold grill to full heat without cracking, and it absorbs moisture from the dough efficiently, which is what gives you that crispy underside instead of something pale and soft. Standard ceramic works, but it's more fragile under rapid temperature changes.

A glazed surface adds a layer of non-stick behavior that unglazed cordierite doesn't offer naturally. The trade-off is that a heavy glaze can slightly reduce moisture absorption. For most backyard cooks who aren't running their grills at 700°F, that's an acceptable exchange for easier cleanup and less sticking.

### Fit and Grill Compatibility

This is where most buyers get into trouble. Weber's product ecosystem is intentionally tiered , the Crafted Frame Kit, the Gourmet BBQ System, and the standard kettle all use different mounting points and grate configurations. A stone that fits a Genesis 325 won't necessarily drop into a Spirit or a kettle without adaptation.

Measure your grate dimensions before buying. If your grill uses a center grate insert (like the Gourmet BBQ System), you need a stone sized for that insert opening, not the full grate. If you're running a 22-inch kettle, you'll want a kit designed around that diameter. Compatibility errors don't just mean poor fit , they mean uneven heat and, in some cases, unsafe use.

### Preheating and Temperature Management

Every pizza stone requires preheating, and skipping this step is the most common mistake new users make. Cold stone onto hot crust is a recipe for steaming, not baking. Allow at least 20, 30 minutes at your target temperature before the pizza goes on. On a gas grill, that's straightforward. On a charcoal grill, it requires managing your coal arrangement to maintain a consistent temperature zone under the stone.

Indirect heat setups work better for pizza than direct. Banking coals to one side, or running only the outer burners on a gas grill, lets the stone absorb heat evenly without scorching the bottom before the top has a chance to cook through. Getting this heat architecture right is as important as the stone itself. If you're serious about building a grill-based pizza setup, spending time on the full range of [outdoor pizza cooking](/pizza-ovens/) methods will show you what stone placement options work for different grill configurations.

### Size and Practical Fit

Stone size affects both the pizza you can cook and how the stone behaves thermally. A larger stone takes longer to preheat but holds temperature more consistently once it's up to heat. A smaller stone heats faster but drops temperature more noticeably when a cold pizza hits its surface.

For a 22-inch kettle, a stone in the 13, 15 inch range is practical , large enough for a real pizza, small enough to manage airflow around it. For larger gas grills with dedicated frame kits, you can run a bigger stone that covers more of the cooking surface, which gives you more flexibility with crust size and positioning.

## Buying Guide

### Matching the Stone to Your Grill System

The first decision isn't which stone you prefer , it's which stones you're actually eligible to buy. Weber's accessory ecosystem has hard compatibility walls, and buying across them produces frustrating results. The Crafted Frame Kit stones require the Crafted Frame Kit grill models. The Gourmet BBQ System stone requires the center grate insert. The onlyfire kit requires a 22-inch kettle. Start with your grill model and work backward to the compatible options rather than starting with a stone and hoping it fits.

If you're not sure which system your grill uses, look at the center grate section , the Gourmet BBQ System uses a round removable insert, while the Crafted Frame uses a modular rail system along the grill sides. Standard grates without either system point toward the black Weber stone or the onlyfire kit.

### Cordierite vs. Glazed Ceramic

Material choice matters for frequency of use. Cordierite is worth the premium if you're cooking pizza regularly , monthly or more often. Its thermal shock resistance means it handles repeated heat cycling without developing cracks over time. For occasional use, a glazed ceramic stone performs adequately and costs less.

The glazed finish question cuts across both materials. Glaze makes cleanup dramatically easier and reduces sticking with raw doughs. Unglazed surfaces absorb more moisture from the dough, which can produce a slightly crisper bottom , the difference most home cooks will notice in blind testing is smaller than the reviews suggest.

### What the Kettle Crowd Actually Needs

Kettle grill owners face a different configuration challenge than gas grill owners. The kettle's dome lid creates a radiant heat environment that's actually quite good for pizza, but the stock setup doesn't direct heat efficiently around a pizza stone placed in the center. The onlyfire ring kit addresses this by modifying the airflow and dome height simultaneously.

Without a dedicated kit, kettle pizza requires a two-zone coal setup with the stone off-center, which works but demands more active management. Exploring purpose-built [pizza oven accessories for grill setups](/pizza-ovens/) will show you how other backyard cooks handle the kettle pizza challenge with different approaches and equipment.

### Preheating as a Non-Negotiable Step

No stone , regardless of material, brand, or price band , delivers good results without adequate preheating. Budget at least 20 minutes at your target temperature, 30 minutes if you're running a charcoal setup where temperature management is less precise. Putting a pizza on a stone that hasn't reached cooking temperature produces a pale, doughy crust with a bottom that's steamed rather than baked.

An infrared thermometer is the right tool for verifying stone surface temperature. Grill thermometers measure air temperature in the dome, not surface temperature on the stone , the two can differ significantly. A reading of 500, 550°F at the stone surface is a reasonable target for most home pizza on a gas grill.

### Peel and Landing Technique

A stone without a peel is an incomplete setup. Sliding a pizza off a wooden or aluminum peel onto a hot stone is a skill that takes two or three attempts to get right , too much flour and the crust bakes dry underneath, too little and the pizza sticks mid-transfer and folds on itself. Use semolina rather than flour on the peel surface; it acts more like ball bearings and releases cleanly.

Build the pizza on the peel, work quickly, and use a forward-and-back shake motion to confirm it's sliding before you commit to the launch. If it's stuck, slide the peel out carefully, dust more semolina underneath, and retry. The aluminum peel included with the onlyfire kit is adequate for learning; a longer-handled peel gives more clearance from the grill's heat if you're cooking regularly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a special pizza stone made for Weber grills, or will any pizza stone work?

A generic pizza stone will work on a Weber grill as long as it fits your grate configuration, but Weber-specific stones designed for the Gourmet BBQ System or Crafted Frame Kit integrate more cleanly and sit more securely. For kettle owners, the [onlyfire Stainless Steel Pizza Ring Kit](product:onlyfire-stainless-steel-pizza-ring-kit) solves the fit problem while also addressing dome height, which a random stone dropped on the grate does not. Compatibility matters more than brand loyalty here.
### How long does it take to preheat a pizza stone on a Weber grill?

Plan on 20, 30 minutes at your target cooking temperature before the pizza goes on. Gas grills reach stable stone temperatures faster than charcoal setups, where managing coal distribution adds variability. An infrared thermometer aimed at the stone surface is more reliable than your grill's dome thermometer, which reads air temperature rather than surface temperature. The stone should be at 500, 550°F before your first pizza.
### What is the difference between the Weber Gourmet BBQ System stone and the Crafted Frame Kit stone?

These are two separate Weber accessory systems with different mounting configurations. The [Weber Gourmet BBQ System Pizza Stone](product:weber-gourmet-bbq-system-pizza-stone) drops into the round center insert used on older Genesis and Spirit models. The [Crafted Glazed Baking Pizza Stone](product:crafted-glazed-baking-pizza-stone) mounts in the rail-based Crafted Frame Kit used on newer Genesis E/S 325/335/435 series grills. They are not interchangeable , confirm which system your grill uses before buying either.
### Is cordierite worth it over standard ceramic for a grill pizza stone?
For regular use, yes. Cordierite handles the repeated thermal cycling , grill to room temperature and back , without developing the hairline cracks that eventually compromise ceramic stones. The [Weber Crafted Glazed Baking Stone , Cordierite](product:weber-crafted-glazed-baking-stone-cordierite) combines that thermal durability with a glazed finish that reduces sticking. If you're making pizza a few times a year, a standard glazed ceramic is fine.
### Can I use a Weber pizza stone on a charcoal grill, or are these only for gas?

Most Weber pizza stones are rated for gas, charcoal, and electric grills, including the [Weber Stone Grill Accessory, Black](product:weber-stone-grill-accessory-black-heat), though you should confirm compatibility for your specific model. The onlyfire kit is designed exclusively for the Weber 22-inch charcoal kettle. On charcoal, the technique requires more attention , a two-zone setup with the stone above indirect heat produces the most consistent results and avoids scorching the bottom before the top cooks through.
